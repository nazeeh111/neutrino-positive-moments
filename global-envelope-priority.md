## Decisive primary antecedents

### Exact beam growth was known in 2016

Sovan Chakraborty, Rasmus Sloth Hansen, Ignacio Izaguirre and Georg Raffelt, *Self-induced neutrino flavor conversion without flavor mixing*, JCAP 03 (2016) 042, arXiv:1602.00698v2, Section 2.2 Eq. (2.10), gives the exact fastest two-beam rate 2 mu sqrt(1-a^2). Section 2.3 Eqs. (2.14)-(2.16) gives explicit four-mode frequencies, an instability band, and its maximizing wave number and growth rate. Their Eq. (2.14) also supplies an old example of finite-wave-number instability despite homogeneous stability.

Source inspected: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1602.00698 ; DOI https://doi.org/10.1088/1475-7516/2016/03/042 . These are fixed chosen beam models, not extrema over all positive distributions sharing independently prescribed species moments. The difference is the quantifier and constraints, not the presence of square roots or an optimizing wave number.

### The weighted scalar transverse relation was explicit in 2019

Changhao Yi, Lei Ma, Joshua D. Martin and Huaiyu Duan, *Dispersion relation of the fast neutrino oscillation wave*, Phys. Rev. D 99, 063005 (2019), arXiv:1901.01546v2, Section III Eqs. (34)-(35), gives

    D_AS=(I0+1)(I2-1)-I1^2,
    D_SB=I0-I2-2.

Equation (43a) explicitly writes the symmetry-breaking relation using G(u)(1-u^2). This makes the nonnegative weight and its vanishing at the angular endpoints an existing structural feature, even if the new argument exploits it differently. Their analysis concerns critical points and branches for specified distributions. No optimization over the positive per-species M0/M1/M2 uncertainty class was found in the inspected text.

### The unweighted signed-ELN geometric-mean estimate was explicit in 2022

The text explicitly calls this an estimate and attributes the scaling to Morinaga et al. (2020). Equation (24) then approximates it from reconstructed maximum-entropy angular distributions. ## How narrow the defensible new result is

For the fixed-k theorem, the potentially new content is the proof that the maximum over all real frequency parts and all compatible positive measures equals the displayed closed form, with explicit attainers. The proof's cancellation of the real-frequency dependence earns more than a frequency scan, but does not make Weyl disks new.

For supplied Q_s, define weighted positive measures dH_s=(1-u^2)dmu_s. Their masses are R_s=N-Q_s. Under zero signed zeroth and first moments, the longitudinal equation reduces to a scalar Cauchy-transform equation with this weight; the transverse equation has the existing factor of one half. Thus the square-root product can be understood through ordinary positive-transform bounds and classical two-beam algebra. The substantive step is showing these bounds remain exactly attainable while obeying the original separate species mass, flux and second-moment constraints. Endpoint compensation atoms, invisible to H_s, provide the mechanism. That exact moment-preserving sharpness, with exceptions, is the priority target.

Do not claim that an extremizing completion is the actual radiation field, or that every compatible completion grows at this rate. Do not extend an axial result to arbitrary three-dimensional distributions. Do not call number second moments thermodynamic pressure without an energy assumption. Exact atomic attainment is in the measure class; smooth-density attainment requires its own statement. The Q_plus=Q_minus=0 exception is essential, as is the R_plus R_minus=0 no-growth case. Large optimizing k near a degenerate moment boundary establishes no physical spatial resolution or local-model validity.
