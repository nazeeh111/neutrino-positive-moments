# Preimplementation gates: exact positive-moment frequency uncertainty

2026-11-01, before implementation/results for this candidate. This is not a relaxation of the previous L2-tail pilot: that cost/indispensability gate remains failed. Here the new question is exact attainable frequencies without an angular-tail prior.

Scope: axisymmetric angular measures on u in[-1,1], k>0 along the symmetry axis, local collisionless fast limit. Species numbers/fluxes are given exactly. Use electron neutrino and antineutrino with pointwise heavy-species cancellation as an explicit model assumption. Positive measures may include atoms. Longitudinal and both equal transverse sectors must be covered. Every feasibility claim is existential for one frequency; no universal-instability or all-k result follows.

StageA: derive and implement the rational reweighted moment LMIs. Verify random rational atomic measures, roundtrip transform/quad witnesses and both dispersion-sector algebraic reductions against direct beam matrices. Feasible claims need exact rational PSD/moment checks and an attaining measure representation; infeasible claims need a PSD rational separating certificate, not an optimizer status. Degenerate zero-mass and unit-flux-factor measures require explicit handling. Invalid inputs refuse. Audit numerical proposals against independent direct discrete-angle LPs and exact analytic controls; no false positive/negative permitted. Solver failure returns unresolved.

StageB proposed screen, only afterA correctness: three moment cases (a) bothspecies mass1,flux0; (b) mass1each,flux+1/3 and-1/3, the published fiducial axial projection; (c) single positive species mass1,flux1/3, second species0. k=1 andk=1/2. Frequencies Reomega in{-3/2,-1,-1/2,0,1/2,1,3/2}; Imomega in{1/20,1/10,1/5,2/5,4/5,6/5}; both longitudinal/transverse sectors:504 classifications. All data/locations fixed; no post-outcome selection.

Require at least90% exact certified decisions (positive or negative) at the nondegenerate sampled points; all single-species points must be certified impossible or unresolved, never possible; all directly feasible LP completions must be consistent with the continuous method (no certified impossibility). For the identical zero-flux M1 case, require at least4 exact attained unstable frequencies at nonzero k although a valid identical-species/maxentropy completion has G=0 and is stable. That demonstrates necessary abstention rather than a manufactured universal instability claim. This zero-flux case has no robust zero-mode lower growth guarantee, so it cannot be passed by continuity of such a guarantee.

Strong comparisons: exact known zero-mode interval criterion, ordinary polynomial crossing tests where relevant, maxentropy closure as ONE completion, angular-grid LP feasibility at32,128,512 nodes (inner approximation, not a continuous impossibility oracle), and generic conic feasibility if available without disproportionate setup. Time preprocessing/solver/proof checking separately. No speedup claim against only a huge discrete eigenproblem. Exact continuous feasibility and checkable extrema are the intended potential new capability, not claim that old Weyl-circle or SDP algorithms are new.

A surviving screen still needs primary-priority review, broader physical cases with number-moment provenance, generic conic/known Weyl-circle algorithm comparisons, precision/stability/scaling, interval coverage if claiming regions or extrema, independent adversarial review and useful scientific insight beyond known closure ambiguity. No publication-readiness or high-impact claim from504 points alone. Reject/revise explicitly if the map is already implemented, exact certification collapses near useful boundaries, or no capability beyond strong conventional bounds is demonstrated. Preserve every failed/unresolved case.
