# Physical scope and terminology corrections

All prescribed moments here are NUMBER-weighted angular moments. N denotes a dimensionless self-interaction-weighted number mass: choose a reference number densityN0 and frequencymu0=sqrt(2)*G_F*N0; then masses are Ns/N0, frequenciesomega/mu0 and wave numbersc*k/mu0. The code absorbs these common factors. N=k=1 is a dimensionless test, not a specified astrophysical density or wavelength.

The smooth profiles share second-moment tensorN*I/3 in NUMBER weighting. Earlier task notes and the pinned smooth plan used 'pressure' loosely. Radiation pressure is energy weighted. The two coincide up to a common energy factor only for monoenergetic radiation or an explicitly shared angle-independent energy spectrum. The validated numerical model does not supply an energy-dependent transport pressure. This clarification changes no input, proof or gate; the historical pinned plan is preserved rather than silently edited.

The dispersion variablesomega,k are reduced by real matter/self-interaction current shifts. Those shifts depend on fixed monopole/dipole data and are common to all completions in each moment class. 'Zero mode' in general means shiftedk=0. Equal-density,zero-flux examples have vanishing signed self-interaction shifts; with static matter or its known real phase removed, the example maps directly to the corresponding physical temporal growth. Purely imaginary means the reduced-frequency imaginary axis. A separate proved extension now bounds growth over every real reduced frequency for the equal-mass/zero-flux fixed-k class; equality is on that axis. Supplied separate second number moments also yield a sharp maximum over all k, with stable corners. These special theorems do not classify general frequency regions or arbitrary moment classes.

Only axisymmetric angular distributions with wave vector along that axis are covered by the exact four-disk algorithm. The constructed smooth distributions are legitimate smooth functions onS², but this does not make the uncertainty upper bounds valid for arbitrary nonaxisymmetric angular distributions.

Pointwise equality of heavy-flavor neutrino/antineutrino distributions is an explicit model assumption when cancelling them. Equal moments alone do not imply it. Species distributions are positive angular measures; microscopic energy-resolved occupancy bounds and physical transport error are not imposed. Possible means some completion in this specified information class, not a prediction that the actual background is unstable. Smoothness checks remove a singular-measure loophole but do not establish astrophysical relevance or practical transport superiority.
