# Complete equal-second-moment envelope: priority update

2026-11-01. The new contribution under assessment is the complete sharp finite-band envelope for positive axial species with individual equal moments(N,0,Na), including all real frequencies, endpoint/cubic/plateau transitions and positive attainers. It is not a claim to have invented Weyl circles, completing squares, convex combinations of disk inequalities, endpoint beam dispersion, moment-based diagnosis or an astrophysical cutoff.

Primary-source refresh found a relevant earlier moment-only growth diagnostic that should be cited explicitly: Dasgupta, Mirizzi and Sen, Physical Review D98,103001(2018), DOI10.1103/PhysRevD.98.103001. The arXiv version is titled A few moments to diagnose fast flavor conversions of supernova neutrinos; the journal title is Simple method of diagnosing fast flavor conversions of supernova neutrinos. https://arxiv.org/html/1807.03322v2 , SectionIII, equations13-20, derives the reduced zero-mode dispersion solely from the signed second-moment tensor. Equations9-10 specify real matter/current shifts. This is a direct precedent for moment-only growth calculations, not a general maximum over all angular completions and wave numbers. In the present equal per-species moment class, that signed tensor vanishes, so its reduced zero mode is stable. The theorem concerns nonzero reduced k and a worst-case completion class, rather than improving their prediction of that zero mode. This distinction follows from their equations and the stated moment constraints; their paper does not validate the new theorem's usefulness.

The existing source record also includes Chakraborty etal2016 exact beam maxima, Yi etal2019 axial weighting, Richers2022 approximate instability metrics, Johns/Nagakura2021 completion ambiguity, and diDio2015/Heideetal2024 classical transform loci. None is relabeled an invention. The present result specializes these classical tools and adds an all-real-frequency enclosing-disk argument, followed by global optimization and explicit moment-preserving attainment. The bounded refreshed search used fast flavor instability + second moments/growth bounds/fixed moments. It did not locate an identical complete constrained envelope. That is limited search evidence, not proof of priority or independent human peer review.

The research repository is public; no external expert correspondence or paper submission has occurred. The failed M2 counterflux utility gate and typically faster continuous-conic comparison remain material limits. The new theorem fills a mathematical gap and supplies an exact rational evaluator. It does not establish that the restricted result is high impact or that the full moonshot goal has been achieved.
