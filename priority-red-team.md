## Verdict

**The evidence does not yet establish that the result is genuinely novel or scientifically useful.** It does support a narrower statement: the complete equal-second-moment, finite-wave-number sharp envelope is a plausible specialized theorem contribution, and this bounded attack found no source giving its identical quantifiers, three regimes and attaining measures. This is a priority hypothesis, not a priority finding.

The exact frequency classifier has much weaker novelty footing. Its decisive reduction is an application of classical fixed-point moment-transform loci; positivity, finite moment matrices, low-support extremizers, convex dual certificates and optimization over all compatible distributions are established methods. Combining two species and checking four disks does not constitute a new general moment algorithm. Its exact-boundary implementation can still be useful software, but the existing conic timing loss and failed physical-use gate prevent treating it as demonstrated high-impact algorithmic progress.

No claim should be upgraded merely because the proofs survived model-agent review, the source is public, or a search found no matching title. Those establish different facts.

## Strongest direct collision: classical moment-transform geometry

The retrieved [di Dio paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.06589), *Weyl Circles for one-dimensional Moment Problems*, gives the finite-interval transform locus in **Proposition5.1** and its circle formulas in **Theorem5.2**, on PDF pages8–9. Proposition5.1 says that varying the representing measure at fixed complex z fills a region bounded by two circular arcs; it attributes that fact to earlier moment literature. Section6 also describes intersecting circles for combined support restrictions. This directly precedes the species transform geometry.

Its header identifies arXiv1506.06589v1 while its footer bears April10,2021. Cite Proposition5.1/Theorem5.2 with the version actually used; a thesis or another version may have different numbering. The 2024 Heide/Fritzsche/Kirstein/Mädler two-ball result is already a correctly identified direct predecessor, but it does not make the older scalar geometry newly invented.

Inference from the mapping: once the axial dispersion relation is reduced to a required difference of two species Stieltjes values, a fixed-frequency feasibility test from these loci is structurally immediate. The specialized rational implementation, degenerate-case handling and independent replay are engineering contributions. An additional scientific capability must be demonstrated separately.

## Additional collision: optimizing over every compatible distribution is old

[Bertsimas and Popescu, SIAM J. Optimization15(3),780–804(2005)](https://www.mit.edu/~dbertsim/papers/MomentProblems/Optimal-inequalities-in-probability-theory-A-convex-optimization-approach-SIAM15.pdf), DOI10.1137/S1052623401399903, explicitly formulates optimal bounds over distributions with prescribed moments. See equations2.1–2.2, weak/strong duality Theorems2.1–2.2, the discussion following Theorem2.2 of discrete extremizers, and Section3's univariate semidefinite formulation. Their objective is an event probability, not neutrino spectral growth. Thus this is a strong methodological collision, not an identical growth theorem. It rules out claiming that a universal moment-class optimization, an atomic attainer or an SDP certificate is itself the novel mechanism.

A general method that could solve a specialization does not automatically establish priority for a particular closed form. Conversely, a new closed form does not automatically establish an important new algorithm. The finite-band result must be assessed at that more precise level.

## Physics predecessors and distinct quantifiers

[Johns, Nagakura, Fuller and Burrows, PRD101,043009(2020)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1910.05682), arXiv1910.05682v2, develops angular-moment dynamics and instability criteria. Equations1–5 use homogeneous, axially symmetric evolution; Figure3 studies families with prescribed higher multipoles set to zero. Its Fornax example in Figure2 uses M1-derived pressures/heat fluxes and adjusts the species-density ratio to trigger instability. It is a direct predecessor for moment-based physics, but it is not the present supremum over all positive completions and all real frequencies at each finite k. That distinction is substantive and should be explicit.

[Dasgupta, PRL128,081102(2022)](https://arxiv.org/html/2110.00192), arXiv2110.00192v2, proves that collective flavor instability requires a crossing. The proof culminates in equation26; its discussion states the lack of dependence on azimuthal symmetry and notes that crossing does not prescribe a large growth rate. This defeats any claim to a first rigorous instability condition from positivity/sign structure. It does not supply the candidate's exact finite-band upper envelope.

[Fiorillo and Raffelt, *Theory of neutrino fast flavor evolution, Part I*](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.06708), arXiv2406.06708v3, supplies a broader linear-response/initial-value framework. Sections3–4 derive susceptibility and its analyticity; Sections5–6 analyze near-luminal modes and crossings; AppendixA develops the Vlasov/plasma analogy. It addresses a specified angular distribution and the origin of modes, rather than optimization over the positive low-moment completion class. The inspected sections yielded no equivalent endpoint/cubic/plateau theorem. This is a comparison of inspected content, not a comprehensive exclusion of related work.

Existing records already identify Dasgupta/Mirizzi/Sen2018 zero-mode growth, Chakraborty etal2016 beam maxima, Yi etal2019 axial weighting, Richers2022 approximate growth metrics and Johns/Nagakura2021 completion ambiguity. This attack does not undo those acknowledgments or turn a familiar weighted geometric mean into a new physical law.

The new algebraic work can be mathematically nontrivial while remaining scientifically narrow. This distinction must survive any abstract, README or outreach claim.

## Plasma search and negative-search limits

The attack also searched Vlasov/two-stream spectral growth bounds, moment-constrained instability and semicircle language. Retrieved Vlasov “growth estimates” often concerned nonlinear velocity support or derivative norms over time, while two-stream “band” results concerned finite spatial domains and electrode boundary conditions. These are not equivalent to a temporal spectral supremum over measures with fixed angular moments. They were not treated as collisions merely because they contain the words “growth” or “bound.” Nor does their mismatch establish novelty.

Search routes included combinations of neutrino/fast flavor with growth maximum, rigorous upper bound, sharp moments and moment-constrained instability, together with generalized moment optimization, fixed-point Weyl loci and Vlasov/two-stream/semicircle terms. This is bounded web and primary-paper inspection. It does not exhaust conference proceedings, theses, non-English literature, equivalent formulations or unpublished knowledge. The search returned noise and failed HTML routes for some old papers; PDF sources were used where available. No popularity count or lack of search matches is taken as priority evidence.

## What would change the verdict

The most discriminating novelty question is exact: has anyone proved the same extremum over all positive axial species with moments(N,0,Na), at each fixed k and arbitrary real frequency, including the three regimes and moment-preserving attainers? A relevant expert can identify an equivalent theorem or explain why this is only a routine corollary; a general compliment about exact arithmetic would not resolve that issue.

Usefulness needs a separate scientific decision test, with independently specified moments and assumptions that actually fit the model. Excluding a proposed mode that no admissible completion can support could be meaningful. Merely bounding hypothetical growth, confirming already-compatible modes or repeatedly resolving designed rational boundaries does not demonstrate that value. The current failed counter-flux gate and conic comparison remain contrary evidence. This priority audit supplies no new utility pass and no basis for calling the full high-impact goal achieved.
